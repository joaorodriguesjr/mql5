Trade Constants

Various constants used for programming trading strategies are divided into the following groups:

* [History Database Properties](enum_series_info_integer.md) receiving general information on a symbol;

* [Order properties](orderproperties.md) obtaining information about trade orders;
* [Position properties](positionproperties.md) obtaining information about current positions;
* [Deal properties](dealproperties.md) obtaining information about deals;
* [Trade operation types](enum_trade_request_actions.md) description of trade operations available;
* [Trade transaction types](enum_trade_transaction_type.md) - description of possible trade transactions types;
* [Trade orders in DOM](enum_book_type.md) separation of orders according to the direction of a requested operation.
