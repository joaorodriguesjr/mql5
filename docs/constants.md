Constants, Enumerations and Structures

To simplify the program writing and to make program texts more convenient for perception, the MQL5 language provides predefined standard constants and enumerations. Besides that, service [structures](classes.md) are used for storing information.

Standard constants are similar to macros and are of [int](integertypes.md) type.

The constants are grouped by their purposes:

* [Chart constants](chartconstants.md) are used when working with price charts: opening, navigation, setting parameters;
* [Objects constants](objectconstants.md) are intended for processing graphical objects that can be created and displayed in charts;
* [Indicators constants](indicatorconstants.md) are used for working with standard and custom indicators;

* [Environment state](environment_state.md) constants describe properties of a MQL5-program, show information about a client terminal, financial instrument and current account;

* [Trade constants](tradingconstants.md) allow to specify a variety of information in the course of trading;
* [Named constants](namedconstants.md) are constants of the MQL5 language;
* [Data structures](structures.md) describe data storage formats used;
* [Codes of errors and warnings](errorswarnings.md) describe compiler messages and trading server answers to trade requests;
* [In/out constants](io_constants.md) are designed for working with [file functions](files.md) and displaying messages on the screen by the [MessageBox()](messagebox.md) function.
